## Marketplace starter packs

Both are public domain (CC0 1.0). Credit is given as a courtesy.

- `marketplace/packs/office-kc1000`: "Keyboard Soundpack #1" by unicaegames,
  <https://opengameart.org/content/keyboard-soundpack-1-typing-and-single-keystrokes>. The 32 single-key WAV files are unchanged.
- `marketplace/packs/tactile-mix`: Freesound HQ previews, unchanged, from
  StavSounds (sounds 766628–766640), alpinemesh (627647), yottasounds (380141) and Foxfire- (570755).
  Per-file links are in the pack's `pack.json`.
